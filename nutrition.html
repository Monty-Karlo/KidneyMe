Understanding Kidney Nutrition

The kidneys help regulate different substances in the body. Depending on a person's kidney function and medical needs, a healthcare professional may recommend changes to their diet.

Sodium

Sodium is a mineral commonly found in salt and many packaged or processed foods. Some people with kidney disease may be advised to monitor their sodium intake.

Potassium

Potassium is an important mineral found in many foods. The amount a person needs can depend on their kidney function, medications, and other health factors.

Phosphorus

Phosphorus is another mineral found naturally in many foods and added to some processed foods. Kidney function can affect how the body manages phosphorus.

Fluids

Fluid needs are different for everyone. Some people with kidney problems may need to monitor how much fluid they consume based on guidance from their healthcare provider.

Nutrition Tips

Read Food Labels

Nutrition labels can help users understand the nutrients contained in packaged foods.

Ask Your Healthcare Team

Kidney nutrition needs can be different for each person. A doctor or registered dietitian can provide recommendations based on individual needs.

Plan Ahead

Planning meals ahead of time can make it easier to keep track of foods and follow recommendations from a healthcare professional.

Medical Disclaimer: Dietary needs are different for each person. Kidney and Me provides educational information only. Talk with a healthcare professional or registered dietitian before making major changes to your diet.
