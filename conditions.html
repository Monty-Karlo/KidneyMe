Chronic Kidney Disease

Chronic kidney disease, also called CKD, happens when the kidneys are damaged and have difficulty filtering blood properly over a long period of time.

Common Symptoms

Kidney disease may not always cause noticeable symptoms during its early stages. Symptoms can become more noticeable as kidney function decreases.

  • Feeling tired
  • Swelling in the feet or ankles
  • Changes in urination
  • Difficulty concentrating
Causes and Risk Factors

Different health conditions can increase a person's risk of developing chronic kidney disease. A healthcare provider can help determine individual risk factors.

Treatment and Management

Treatment depends on the cause and stage of kidney disease. Healthcare providers may recommend changes involving medications, diet, lifestyle, or other treatments.
