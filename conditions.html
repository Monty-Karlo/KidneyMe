Chronic Kidney Disease

Chronic kidney disease, also called CKD, is a condition where the kidneys become damaged and have difficulty filtering blood as well as they should.

Common Symptoms

People with early kidney disease may not notice symptoms. Symptoms can become more noticeable as kidney function decreases.

  • Feeling tired
  • Swelling in the feet or ankles
  • Changes in urination
  • Difficulty concentrating
Causes and Risk Factors

Several health conditions can increase the risk of developing chronic kidney disease. More detailed information will be added as the website develops.

Treatment and Management

Treatment depends on the cause and stage of kidney disease. A healthcare professional can recommend treatment based on a person's individual condition.
