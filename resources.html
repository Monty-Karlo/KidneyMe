Trusted Resources

These organizations provide reliable kidney and general health information. External links open the organizations' websites.

NIDDK

The National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases provides information about CKD, kidney failure, kidney stones, nutrition, testing, and treatment.

Visit NIDDK

MedlinePlus

MedlinePlus is a service of the National Library of Medicine with patient-friendly health information and links to trusted medical resources.

Visit MedlinePlus

National Kidney Foundation

The National Kidney Foundation provides education, patient support, kidney-health information, and resources for people living with kidney disease.

Visit National Kidney Foundation

CDC

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention provides information about chronic kidney disease, risk factors, prevention, and public health.

Visit CDC Kidney Disease

Sources used for Kidney and Me

The educational content in this project was written from information provided by NIDDK and MedlinePlus, with additional trusted resources provided for continued learning. Users should follow advice from their own healthcare professionals for individual medical decisions.
