Medications and Kidney Health

The kidneys help remove many substances from the body. Kidney function can sometimes affect how medications are processed, which makes it important to discuss medications with a healthcare professional.

Medication Safety

Keep an updated list of medications and share it with healthcare providers. This can include prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements.

Prescription Medications

Prescription medications should be taken according to the instructions provided by a healthcare professional. Patients should ask questions if they are unsure why a medication was prescribed or how it should be taken.

Over-the-Counter Medications

Over-the-counter medicines can still affect the body and may not be appropriate for everyone with kidney problems. Ask a healthcare professional before taking a new medicine if you have concerns about kidney health.

Questions to Ask Your Doctor
  • Why am I taking this medication?
  • How often should I take it?
  • Could this medication affect my kidneys?
  • Are there medications or supplements I should avoid?
  • What should I do if I miss a dose?

Medication Tips

Keep a Medication List

Keep a current list of medications so it can be shared during medical appointments.

Follow Instructions

Follow the instructions provided by your healthcare professional and ask questions when something is unclear.

Ask Before Making Changes

Talk with a healthcare professional before starting, stopping, or changing medications.
