Use medicines safely

Medications

Kidney function can affect how medicines are handled by the body. Keep your healthcare team informed about prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and supplements.

Medication Safety

Keep an updated medication list

Bring an updated list of medicines and supplements to healthcare visits. A pharmacist can also help check for interactions and kidney-related concerns.

Be careful with over-the-counter medicines

Some NSAID pain relievers, including ibuprofen and naproxen, can damage kidneys in certain situations. People with CKD or kidney risk factors should ask a healthcare professional or pharmacist which OTC products are appropriate for them.

Plan ahead for illness

Vomiting, diarrhea, fever, or poor fluid intake can cause dehydration. Ask your healthcare professional ahead of time what to do with your medicines if you become sick.

Questions to ask
  • Why am I taking this medicine?
  • Does my kidney function affect the dose?
  • Could this medicine affect my kidneys?
  • Which OTC medicines or supplements should I avoid?
  • What should I do with my medicines if I become dehydrated or sick?
Important: Do not start, stop, or change a prescribed medicine based on this website. Contact your healthcare professional or pharmacist for individual advice.
